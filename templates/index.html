Capabilities

Everything a clinical team needs.

From raw patient data to actionable risk insight — in seconds.

Ensemble Intelligence

Three models — Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost — vote together with calibrated weights. No single model's blind spots dominate the decision.

Explainable Predictions

Every prediction comes with an AI-generated clinical summary explaining which patient factors drove the result, in plain language a doctor can act on.

Recall-Optimized

In healthcare, missing a high-risk patient is worse than a false alarm. Our threshold is tuned to catch 75% of patients who will be readmitted — not just maximize accuracy.

Visual Risk Profiling

Radar charts and risk gauges show where a patient sits relative to the population across all clinical dimensions at a single glance.

Patient History

All past predictions are stored locally so you can track risk trends across multiple patients and sessions without any server-side storage.

Printable Reports

Generate a clean, print-ready PDF summary of any prediction for inclusion in patient notes or handover documentation.

Process

Three steps to a risk assessment.

No training required. Enter the patient's data and get a result in under 10 seconds.

1

Enter Patient Data

Fill in the guided 3-step form with demographics, hospital visit metrics, and clinical test results. Every field includes a plain-language tooltip.

2

AI Analysis

Your data passes through feature engineering, IQR normalization, and a soft-voting ensemble of three calibrated models trained on 25,000 real records.

3

Review & Act

Get an immediate risk classification, probability score, radar chart, and an AI-written clinical summary with recommended follow-up actions.

Model Performance

Optimized for what matters.

We chose to maximize recall over raw accuracy. In a clinical context, a false negative — missing a high-risk patient — carries far greater cost than a false alarm.
